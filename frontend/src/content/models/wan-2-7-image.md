Wan 2.7 Image Pro is one of the most exciting additions to the AI image generation landscape. As a multimodal vision-language model, it boasts an incredible understanding of complex prompts and spatial relationships.

For virtual influencer creation, Wan 2.7 is highly effective for establishing initial character sheets and placing personas into highly specific, complex environments.

## Strengths

- **Prompt Adherence:** Wan 2.7 follows complex, multi-subject prompts exceptionally well. If you ask for specific clothing on the left and a specific prop on the right, it generally succeeds where other models blend concepts.
- **Multimodal Capabilities:** It processes both text and image inputs with high fidelity, making it excellent for variations and styling.
- **Text Rendering:** Unlike older models, Wan 2.7 is significantly better at generating legible text within the image (e.g., on t-shirts or neon signs).
- **High-Fidelity Textures:** Produces highly realistic skin textures, fabric details, and lighting.

## Limitations (Honest Assessment)

- **Style Bias:** It can sometimes default to a slightly "hyper-polished" or digital art style unless explicitly prompted for photorealism or raw photography.
- **Speed:** While fast, it is slightly slower than turbo models like Flux Schnell, prioritizing quality over pure speed.

## How to Use Wan 2.7 on Creatify AI

Wan 2.7 is available in the [Creatify AI Image Generator](/tools/creator/image-generator).

1. **Select Model:** Choose "Wan 2.7 Image Pro" from the model dropdown.
2. **Craft your prompt:** Be descriptive. Wan 2.7 thrives on details. Specify lighting, camera angle, and exact clothing details.
3. **Generate:** The model will produce high-resolution outputs suitable for social media.
